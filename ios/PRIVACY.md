# 卡溜趴 隱私權政策

最後更新:2026 年 10 月 11 日

卡溜趴是一個離線優先的旅遊記帳與行程 App。**我們不蒐集任何個人資料。**

- **沒有帳號、沒有伺服器。** App 不需要註冊或登入,也沒有我們的伺服器在接收你的資料。
- **沒有分析工具、沒有廣告。** App 不含任何追蹤、分析或廣告程式。
- **資料只存在你的裝置上。** 旅程、支出、行程、筆記、照片都存在這支手機或平板裡。刪除 App 就會刪除這些資料(除非你自己做過備份)。
- **只有你主動分享時,資料才會離開裝置。** 「分享給同伴」「傳給記帳人」「備份全部資料」會產生一個 .zip 檔,由你透過系統分享視窗(例如 LINE、AirDrop、檔案)傳出去;傳給誰、存到哪裡由你決定。
- **參考匯率。** 開啟「自動帶入參考匯率」時(預設開啟,可在設定關閉),App 每天最多一次向 `open.er-api.com`(ExchangeRate-API)下載公開的匯率表。這個請求不包含你的任何資料、旅程內容或裝置識別碼;如同任何網路連線,對方伺服器會看到你的 IP 位址。關閉此設定後,App 不會連上任何網路服務。
- **收據與手寫辨識在裝置上完成。** 拍收據、手寫記帳使用 iOS 內建的文字辨識,照片與文字不會上傳。
- **相機與相簿。** 相機只在你按「拍收據」「拍照」時使用;「回憶照片自動存到相簿」開啟時,App 只會把照片「加入」你的相簿,不會讀取相簿裡的其他照片。從相簿選照片時,App 只拿得到你選的那幾張。
- **Siri 與捷徑。** 「記一筆」捷徑在你的裝置上執行,把支出寫進 App 的本機資料。
- **兒童隱私。** App 不蒐集任何人的資料,包含兒童。

如果這份政策有變動,會更新在這個頁面。

有任何問題,請到專案的 GitHub 頁面開 issue:<https://github.com/jaykuo0619/travel-ledger/issues>

# 卡溜趴 (TravelLedger) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

卡溜趴 is an offline-first travel expense and itinerary app. **We do not collect any personal data.**

- **No account, no server.** The app needs no sign-up or sign-in, and there is no server of ours receiving your data.
- **No analytics, no ads.** The app contains no tracking, analytics or advertising code.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Trips, expenses, plans, notes and photos are stored on this iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them (unless you made your own backup).
- **Data leaves the device only when you share it.** Sharing a trip, sending additions and backing up produce a .zip file that you send through the system share sheet (for example LINE, AirDrop or Files); you decide who receives it and where it is kept.
- **Reference exchange rates.** While "自動帶入參考匯率" is on (the default; it can be turned off in Settings), the app downloads a public table of exchange rates from `open.er-api.com` (ExchangeRate-API) at most once a day. The request contains none of your data, trip contents or device identifiers; as with any network connection, that server sees your IP address. With the setting off, the app contacts no network service at all.
- **Receipt and handwriting recognition happen on the device.** They use iOS's built-in text recognition; photos and text are not uploaded.
- **Camera and photo library.** The camera is used only when you tap 拍收據 or 拍照. When "回憶照片自動存到相簿" is on, the app only adds photos to your library and cannot read your other photos. When you pick photos, the app receives only the ones you choose.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** The 記一筆 shortcut runs on your device and writes the expense into the app's local data.
- **Children's privacy.** The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
